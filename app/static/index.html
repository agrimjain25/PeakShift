        How the decisions are made
        
      
        
        
        Renewable storage
        When predicted demand is above 160 kW, or the hour is between 18:00 and 22:59.
        
        
        Main grid
        When demand is 160 kW or less and the hour is outside that window.
        
        
        Critical overload
        When demand reaches 200 kW. This is separate from the source and can happen in either.
        
        
        Battery model
        Solar covers the load first. In storage hours the battery covers the rest down to its reserve; the grid helps if it runs out. In grid hours spare solar charges it, then the grid tops it up while total draw stays under 160 kW. The figures come from the settings on the left.
        
        
        Likely range and certainty
        The band is the typical model error of about 10 kW either side. "Sure about source" is the chance the call stays the same if the true demand differs by that much.
